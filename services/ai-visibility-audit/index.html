Is your website visible to AI assistants?

More and more people ask ChatGPT, Claude or Perplexity instead of searching. Those assistants read websites through their own crawlers, and most sites have never checked what those crawlers are allowed to see. Good to know

Is this safe for my website?

Yes. The check reads your public pages the way a careful visitor would, respects the rules your site publishes for crawlers, stays on your own website, and never uses passwords or private areas. It does not change anything on your site.

What can it not see?

Pages that require a login, sites that block automated visitors entirely, and content that only appears after scripts run in a browser. When that happens the report says so instead of guessing.

Will this improve my ranking?

We do not promise that. The report shows what AI crawlers can and cannot do on your site today, so that it is a decision, not an accident. Some items are hygiene, not growth levers, and the report labels them that way.
